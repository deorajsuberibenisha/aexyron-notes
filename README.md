# Aexyron Notes

A handwriting and PDF note app: write with a pen or finger, open many PDFs at once, scan pages into searchable PDFs, planners, templates, stickers and more. Your notes stay on your device.

Open the app: https://deorajsuberibenisha.github.io/aexyron-notes/

On iPad or iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen to install it.
